What you are looking at

A real x86 operating system running on an emulated PC. Nothing here is a web page: the pixels are produced by a 32-bit kernel that was written in L++ and compiled to a flat binary sitting on the disk image below.

Boot chain

  1. 1 BIOS loads our 512-byte boot sector
  2. 2 Stage 2 loads the kernel, sets a VESA mode, enters protected mode
  3. 3 kmain brings up heap, PS/2, PIT, RTC
  4. 4 samar_main — the L++ shell — takes over
